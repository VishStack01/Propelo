What a listing means

  1. Listed means allowed, not promised. The employer holds a licence to sponsor. Whether one role is sponsored is the employer's choice: ask before you apply.
  2. Not listed means it can't sponsor that route today. In the UK you may still work there on a Graduate visa, which needs no sponsor.
  3. Names differ. Registers use legal names ("Monzo Bank Ltd"). Try the shortest form of the name, and check the town.
  4. Pay matters too. A UK Skilled Worker visa needs the role to meet a salary floor. Propelo shows it on each UK role that states pay.

Sources: GOV.UK register of licensed sponsors and the IND public register. This is information, not immigration advice.
