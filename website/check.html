Before you reply to any offer

  1. Never pay to get a job. No registration, training, kit, visa, "refundable" or verification fee. Real employers pay you.
  2. Apply on the company's own website. Find the careers page yourself and check the role is listed there.
  3. Keep your documents. Don't send your passport, an ID number (SSN, National Insurance, PPS, BSN, Aadhaar), bank details or a one-time code before a written offer from a company address.
  4. Slow down. "Only 3 seats left", "no interview" and "join today" are pressure tactics.

Lost money? Call your bank first, then report it:

  • UK: Report Fraud, 0300 123 2040 (in Scotland, Police Scotland on 101)
  • US: reportfraud.ftc.gov
  • Ireland: your local Garda station
  • Netherlands: Fraudehelpdesk, 088 786 73 72
  • India: call 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in
