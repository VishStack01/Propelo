Privacy notice

What we store

  • Your account: name, email, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), sign-in sessions and security events such as sign-ins and password changes.
  • Your job search: the filters you set, the roles matched to you and what you did with them, notes, and the history of each application.
  • Your application details: what you enter on the setup page (contact details, links, experience, notice period, pay expectations, education, work authorization) and the resume files you upload.
  • Where you found us: if the link you followed carried a short label (for example "reddit" or a partner's name), we keep that label with your account to count which channels work. It is a label only: no advertising cookies and no tracking across sites.
  • Your plan: which plan you're on, your subscription's status and renewal date, the currency, and the payment provider's subscription and customer references. We never see or store card, UPI or bank details. Plan payments go through Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record and handles tax. It holds payment details under its own privacy policy.
  • Guarantees, if you start one: what the app checked when you started, your progress (counts of applications, weeks and interviews from your pipeline), and your claim. A person on the team reads them when deciding a claim, and the decision is logged.
  • Concierge, if you ask for it: what you tell us in the request (roles, places, current and expected pay, notice period, an optional phone number), the companies proposed and your answers, messages with your partner, and offers you report. While your case is open, your talent partner can read your Propelo profile, resume and pipeline to do the work; every time they open your case it's logged. Partners are Propelo staff bound by these terms.
  • Contacts: the recruiters, hiring managers and other people you add, with your notes about them. Add only details they shared with you or published themselves.
  • Gmail, if you connect it: your Gmail address and an encrypted permission that lets Propelo send email as you. We ask Google only for permission to send; Propelo can't read, search or delete your mail. We keep a copy of each email Propelo sent for you, with where the recipient's address came from.
  • AI usage: how many AI actions you used each month. If you save your own model key, it's encrypted, and only its last four characters are ever shown.

We never ask for or store passwords to job sites, one-time codes, Aadhaar, PAN, passport or bank details, or your date of birth.

How it's used

Only to run your job search: matching roles to your filters, showing your pipeline, preparing applications, and giving your own apply agent (on your computer, with a key you create) the details it puts into application forms. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI actions

When you use an AI action (reading a posting, scoring fit, tailoring a resume or cover letter), the text needed for that action is sent to the model provider: the service's provider on your plan's allowance, or the provider of your own key. Providers process it to answer the request under their API terms. We don't use your data to train models.

Emails sent for you

Automatic recruiter emails are off until you turn them on and approve a template. Propelo then sends from your own Gmail, only to addresses found on a public page, within your plan's daily limit, and never to anyone who opted out. Every email says it was sent with Propelo and links to a page where the recipient can stop emails from all Propelo users. We keep only a one-way hash of opted-out addresses. Disconnecting Gmail in Settings revokes the permission at Google.

Propelo's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use the Gmail permission only to send the emails you set up, never for advertising, and we don't let people read your Gmail data.

Where applications go

Applications are submitted from your computer's browser, by an agent you set up, to the employer's own application page. The employer then holds what you sent under their own privacy policy.

If your consultancy or college gives you your account

When you join through an invite link from a consultancy, college or other organisation, that organisation sees your name, email, when you joined, and your progress: how many roles were matched to you, how many you applied to, your interviews and offers (company and role) and when you were last active. It does not see your resume, profile details, answers, messages or documents. You agree to this on the join page, and you can leave at any time in Settings; leaving stops the sharing at once and your account stays yours on your own plan.

Recruiting agencies with a roles feed receive lists of open job postings from employers' public career pages. Those lists contain no personal data.

Your choices

  • Export: download everything we hold about you from Settings, Account.
  • Delete: deleting your account removes your account, documents, files, keys and sessions right away. Encrypted backups roll over within 30 days.
  • Correct: edit your details at any time.

Cookies

Propelo sets one cookie to keep you signed in, and short-lived ones while you sign in with LinkedIn or connect Gmail. They're HttpOnly and used only by Propelo. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. The pricing page remembers the currency you picked in your browser's storage.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Sessions are HttpOnly cookies, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and every request for your data is checked against your account. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you think your account was misused.

Concierge terms

Version 2026-11-15. These apply only if you ask for Concierge and accept them in the app.

  • What it is. Concierge is an optional service in the Pro and Ultra plans, for jobs in India for now. A talent partner on the Propelo team helps you find roles, applies for you to the companies you approve, follows up, prepares you for interviews and helps you compare offers. Your plan pays for this work.
  • Your approval comes first. Your partner proposes companies and roles in the app. Nothing is sent to a company until you approve it there, and you can decline any role. Your partner never signs in as you on any website and never uses your LinkedIn account.
  • The fee. If you accept an offer from a company you approved through Concierge, within 6 months of approving it, you pay 8% of the offer's yearly fixed pay, and 6% on any part of it above ₹20 lakh, plus GST where it applies. Bonuses, variable pay, stock and joining bonuses aren't counted. Offers from companies you didn't approve through Concierge carry no fee.
  • When you pay. Nothing is charged before you join. Report the offer in the app; your partner checks the offer letter with you; the fee is then split into three equal monthly instalments, the first due 30 days after your joining date, paid by UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay.
  • If the job ends early. If the employer ends your employment within 90 days of joining, the instalments not yet paid are cancelled. If you leave on your own, the fee stays due.
  • Hiring decisions are the employer's. We work hard to get you hired, but we can't promise a job. If you aren't hired, you owe no fee (see the Job Guarantee).
  • Pausing and closing. You can pause or close Concierge at any time in the app. If your plan ends, your case pauses until you're on Pro or Ultra again. The fee still applies to offers from companies you approved before pausing or closing, within the 6 months above.
  • Be truthful. Share correct details about your experience, current pay and offers. Hiding or misstating an offer from a company you approved through Concierge makes the full fee due at once.

Guarantee terms

Version 2026-11-22. The Interview Guarantee and the Job Guarantee aren't part of Pro, Ultra or any plan. Pro and Ultra members can apply for them in the app; a person on our team reviews every application and accepts or declines it with a reason. These terms apply only once you apply and accept them there. We never promise anyone a job: hiring decisions are the employer's.

Interview Guarantee

  • Applying. You must be on Pro or Ultra, with your experience in your Propelo profile. Tell us which roles you're aiming for. The team looks at how many roles Propelo finds for you and decides within 2 working days. Applying costs nothing.
  • Price. If we accept you, you pay $59 once, charged in your own currency where it is offered, plus any tax shown at checkout, within 7 days of acceptance. Your 45 days start when the payment is confirmed. An acceptance that isn't paid within 7 days lapses, and you can apply again.
  • Your part. For the 45 days, keep Pro or Ultra active and send at least 25 applications in at least 3 different calendar weeks, through Propelo (auto-apply included) or marked as applied in the app. Applications to postings Propelo's scam check marks unsafe don't count.
  • Our part. Within the 45 days you are shortlisted for interviews for at least 3 different roles, or you reach a second round for one role. We never promise a job or an offer: hiring decisions are the employer's.
  • What an interview is. A live conversation by phone, video or in person with someone at the employer, or a recruiter acting for it, about a role you applied to. Recruiter screens count. Online tests, assessments and recorded one-way video interviews don't. Interviews from any source count, and an interview invitation you decline counts as an interview. A second round is a further live interview for the same role after the first, or an offer.
  • Your money back. If the 45 days end with your part done, fewer than 3 interviews and no second round, claim in the app within 30 days and confirm the interviews and invitations you had. A person on the team checks the claim within 5 working days and may ask for details. Approved claims get the full amount you paid back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Your plan fees aren't part of the guarantee.
  • When it stops. Once you have 3 interviews or a second round, the guarantee is met and the price isn't refunded. A guarantee bought under earlier terms keeps the promise and price it was bought with. If your plan ends before the 45 days do, it stops without a refund. Each account can be refunded once.

Job Guarantee

  • No upfront payment. The Job Guarantee is our Concierge service with the commitment that you pay nothing until you're placed. Pro and Ultra members apply in the app, for jobs in India for now. We review every application and accept or decline it with a reason; we take on only people we believe we can help.
  • Once accepted. A talent partner works with you until you're hired: finding roles, applying to the companies you approve, preparing you for interviews and helping you compare offers.
  • The fee, only after you're placed. 8% of the yearly fixed pay of the job you accept, and 6% on any part above ₹20 lakh, plus GST where it applies, under the Concierge terms above (three instalments from 30 days after joining; cancelled if the employer ends the job within 90 days). If you aren't placed, you owe nothing.
  • Be truthful. A claim that hides an interview, an invitation or an offer is rejected, and a refund paid on it must be returned.
  • Your other rights stay. These guarantees add to the refunds below and to your rights under consumer law; they don't replace them.

Refunds and cancellation

Cancel any time in Settings, Plan. Cancelling stops future charges; you keep your plan until the end of the month you've paid for, then your account moves to Free with all your data.

Refunds. If you were charged by mistake (a duplicate charge, or a renewal after you cancelled), write to us within 7 days and we refund it in full to the original payment method. If a paid plan didn't work for you in your first 7 days, ask and we'll refund that first payment. Dollar payments are refunded through Dodo Payments. Refunds reach you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

Delivery. Propelo is an online service; nothing is shipped. Paid features switch on as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a minute.
